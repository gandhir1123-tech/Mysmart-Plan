# MySmart Plan 📱💰

**MySmart Plan** is a mobile-first personal finance and daily maintenance accounting web app featuring a modern Glossy Blue Wallet design, clean dual-account tracking, split-bill group management, and multi-account financial peace of mind.

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Brand Identity & Glossy Blue Wallet Logo**:
  * Vibrant Glossy Royal Blue & Cyan Wallet app icon.
  * Crisp high-resolution PWA icons (`icon-512.png`, `icon-192.png`, `favicon.png`, `logo.png`).
* **Split Bill & Group Expense Calculator (👥 Split Bill)**:
  * Enter any restaurant, cab, grocery, or trip bill (e.g., ₹2,400 with 4 friends).
  * Auto-calculates exact per-person shares (₹600 each).
  * Creates individual friend cards with optional friend names (e.g., *Rahul*, *Priya*, *Amit*).
  * 1-Tap Payback Settlement: tap each friend's badge on the exact day they pay you back.
  * 1-Tap WhatsApp Reminder: opens WhatsApp with pre-filled polite payment reminder and UPI details.
* **80% & 100% Category Budget Warning Alerts (🚨 / ⚠️)**:
  * Dynamic warning banners on the Home Dashboard when spending crosses 80% (amber alert) or exceeds 100% (red overspend alert).
  * Category health bars with visual alert pills in Categories Manager.
* **Strict Two-Account Separation**:
  * **Regular Expense:** Daily living, groceries, utilities, and returnable friend/office claims.
  * **Personal Account:** Long-term wealth, SIP investments, commitments, emergency funds, and personal goals.
* **Linked Funds Transfer (`⇄ Transfer`)**:
  * Seamlessly move funds between Regular and Personal accounts without distorting monthly net income or expense totals.
* **Flexible Shortcut Chips**:
  * Quick-tap shortcut chips (Chai, Auto, Lunch, Groceries, SIP) auto-fill description and category with dynamic amount entry.
* **1-Tap Re-Log / Repeat Entry (`⎘`)**:
  * Instantly repeat past recurring expenses for today with one tap.
* **"⏳ Pending Only" Filter Pill**:
  * Quickly isolate unpaid friend splits and office reimbursements in your ledger.
* **Print-Ready PDF Monthly Statement**:
  * Generates clean A4 monthly statements with KPI cards, itemized group splits table, and category breakdown.
* **100% Offline & Private**:
  * All financial data stays strictly on your device using localStorage with JSON backup & restore.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser on your phone, tablet, or desktop.
3. Add to Home Screen (PWA) for a native app experience.
